About

I am a Ph.D. student in AI at KAIST, advised by Edward Choi. My research focuses on applying machine learning to healthcare, with a particular interest in clinical NLP, persona agents, and user simulation. I developed LabTOP, a unified model for lab test outcome prediction on electronic health records, which received the Best Paper Award at CHIL 2025.
